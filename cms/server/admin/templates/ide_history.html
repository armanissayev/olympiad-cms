Review markers

Markers describe recorded activity and help you choose what to inspect. They are not plagiarism findings. Paste sources are not recorded, so their trustworthiness cannot be determined.

How markers are chosen

Paste markers come from recorded editor paste actions. Copy markers identify a keyboard shortcut, not a confirmed clipboard transfer. Large or rapid insertions and slow editing or long pauses are review cues; completion, undo, formatting, accessibility tools and ordinary thinking can explain unusual activity. Missing recording data is shown separately.
